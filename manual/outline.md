# Atelier manual -- page outline (revised, 16 pages)

Format: A6 (105 x 148 mm), portrait, saddle-stitched, 16 pages (4 sheets x 4pp).

| # | Page | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front cover | "Atelier" wordmark, Bathsheva London, one clean line-art hero drawing (front view, no callouts) |
| 2 | Welcome + in the box | Short welcome note. Numbered list: Atelier, USB-C to USB-C cable, this booklet. Small flat-lay line drawing. |
| 3 | Getting to know Atelier | Front view, numbered callouts: 1 nose cone, 2 grille, 3 LED, 4 knob, 5 fins, 6 foot |
| 4 | Getting to know Atelier (cont.) | Underside view, numbered callouts: USB-C port, foot |
| 5 | Charging | Steps 1-3, LED behaviour while charging (cross-refs LED guide) |
| 6 | Power | Turn on / off (hold knob 2 s), what the LED does at each |
| 7 | Pairing | Step-by-step pairing with a phone, hold knob 5 s, LED behaviour |
| 8 | Controls | Knob close-up drawing with arrows: turn, press, hold |
| 9 | Controls (cont.) | Quick-reference table: action -> result, including factory reset (hold 10 s) |
| 10 | LED guide | Full table: LED state -> meaning (all states marked proposed) |
| 11 | Placement and stability | One page: flat-surface drawing, tip-over caution, do/don't in brief |
| 12 | Caring for lacquer and brass | One page: cleaning cloth only, avoid solvents/abrasives, brass patina note |
| 13 | Safety and battery | One page: general safety + charging safety + disposal/recycling note |
| 14 | Troubleshooting | Symptom / likely cause / fix, table format |
| 15 | Specifications, warranty and support | One page: spec table from specs.yaml, warranty length, support contact |
| 16 | Regulatory + back cover | Compliance marks, manufacturer info, WEEE symbol on the inside; Bathsheva London mark on the outside back cover. If the full regulatory text doesn't fit, it moves to a separate safety leaflet and this page carries a pointer to it. |

## Notes
- This is tighter than the 24-page draft: placement/stability, lacquer/brass
  care, and safety/battery are each now a single page as requested.
- Specifications, warranty and support share page 15 -- workable as long as
  the spec table stays compact (it will, once real ODM numbers replace the
  TBDs -- long TBD placeholder text takes more room than a real number).
- Page 16 carries the regulatory content and doubles as the inside of the
  back cover. If final compliance text runs long, it moves to a separate
  leaflet rather than overflowing the booklet.
