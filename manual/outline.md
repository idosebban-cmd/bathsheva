# Atelier manual -- page outline (proposal)

Format: A6 (105 x 148 mm), portrait, saddle-stitched.
Target: 24 pages total (6 sheets x 4pp), including front and back covers.
This is a first pass. Real copy in Phase 2 may push it to 20 or 28 -- any
multiple of 4 works, it just needs to land on one before final layout.

| # | Page | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front cover | "Atelier" wordmark, Bathsheva London, one clean line-art hero drawing (front view, no callouts) |
| 2 | Welcome | Short welcome note, tone-setting. One paragraph. |
| 3 | In the box | Numbered list (speaker, USB-C cable, this booklet, ...) with a small flat-lay style line drawing |
| 4 | Getting to know Atelier | Front view, numbered callouts: 1 nose cone, 2 grille, 3 LED, 4 knob, 5 fins, 6 foot |
| 5 | Getting to know Atelier (cont.) | Underside view, numbered callouts: USB-C port, foot |
| 6 | Charging | Steps 1-3, LED behaviour while charging (cross-refs LED guide) |
| 7 | Power | Turn on / turn off (hold knob 2s), what the LED does at each |
| 8 | Pairing | Step-by-step pairing with a phone, hold knob 5s, LED behaviour |
| 9 | Controls | Knob close-up drawing with arrows: turn, press, hold |
| 10 | Controls (cont.) | Quick-reference table: action -> result |
| 11 | LED guide | Full table: LED state -> meaning, cross-referenced from charging/power/pairing pages |
| 12 | Placement and stability | "Stand on a flat, stable surface" drawing, tip-over caution, keep away from edges |
| 13 | Placement and stability (cont.) | Do / don't examples (soft surface, near water, etc.) |
| 14 | Caring for the lacquer | Cleaning cloth only, avoid solvents/abrasives |
| 15 | Caring for the brass | Natural patina note, what not to use |
| 16 | Safety | General product safety warnings |
| 17 | Battery safety | Charging safety, disposal / recycling note |
| 18 | Troubleshooting | Symptom / likely cause / fix, table format |
| 19 | Troubleshooting (cont.) | Continued if needed, or folds into specifications |
| 20 | Specifications | Full spec table, pulled from specs.yaml |
| 21 | Specifications (cont.) | Materials, dimensions, weight |
| 22 | Warranty and support | Warranty length, how to get help, support email |
| 23 | Regulatory | Compliance marks, manufacturer info, WEEE symbol |
| 24 | Back cover | Bathsheva London mark, minimal |
