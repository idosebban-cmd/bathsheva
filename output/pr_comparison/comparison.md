## Base-firing radiator details

* Round radiator, 44 mm frame / 35 mm radiating: area 962 mm2, against about 1307 mm2 for the 40 x 60 rear oval. It has to pass the collar opening.
* Sound exits through a 37 mm hole in the collar, then out through a 17.0 mm tall honeycomb mesh ring (235 holes, open area 985 mm2 = 1.02 x radiator area). The ring carries the stepped gold nozzle.
* The ring and nozzle lift the body from 18.2 to 33.4 mm off the ground (overall height fixed, so the red body gets 12.4 mm shorter).
* With steel, the most ballast that fits below the driver is 503 g, so the total is 206 g short of the target. Tungsten alloy reaches it in a shorter cup.
