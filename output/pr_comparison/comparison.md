## Base-firing radiator details

* Round radiator, 44 mm frame / 35 mm radiating: area 962 mm2, against about 1307 mm2 for the 40 x 60 rear oval. It has to pass the collar opening.
* Sound exits through a 37 mm hole in the collar, then sideways through a 9.2 mm gap between the collar (21.1 mm) and the foot stub (11.8 mm), which hangs on 3 posts behind the fins. Exit area 962 mm2 = 1 x radiator area.
* That gap lifts the body from 18.2 to 27.4 mm off the ground (overall height fixed, so the red body gets 7.6 mm shorter).
* With steel, the most ballast that fits below the driver is 522 g, so the total is 208 g short of the target. Tungsten alloy reaches it in a shorter cup.
